ENTIA / PRACTICE

Welcome to the Veil

Walk toward each hallway guide to hear their lesson, enter the arena, defeat two rivals and deposit your essence to complete this tutorial.

1. Fight

Click the floor to walk. Select a spell and press Lanzar to aim, then click its target tile. Escape or Cancelar cancels aiming. Prepare again for each cast. Keep moving while aiming and casting. Inmovilizar stops translation but allows turning; Remover parálisis targets your own tile and costs mana. You cannot remove an enemy's immobilization. Bots can remove their own immobilization too. Aim stays free and spells have no target-area indicators. Walk into the return portal to go back to Nix.

2. Collect

Stand on a dropped crystal and use Pick up or your assigned interaction key. If you die, your carried essence drops in separate piles of up to 10. Rivals can steal it; defeat them to recover what they carry.

3. Deposit

The bank appears nearby after two defeats, with a time-based fallback. Stay nearby for a moment and watch essence flow into storage. Walk away to stop the transfer.

Auto-pot repeats a potion while its assigned macro is held. Assign your own keys in the safe workshop before combat, or choose to play entirely from the windows. You can also hold the hotbar buttons. With Auto-pot off, releasing uses one. Holding still consumes potions at full resources. This practice run supplies 100 of each.

On a phone, tap the floor or use the thumbstick to move. Lanzar changes the next tap to a cast; tap Cancelar again to cancel. Use the contextual button to talk or pick up. Open Inventory for your inventory and resources.

This is an offline demonstration. Spells use original RAO particle and animation artwork. Immobilization lasts 3 seconds and mana costs are rescaled for practice. Rewards, combat and essence rules are illustrative; nothing affects your RAO account.
